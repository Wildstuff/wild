# Hero scene — From a folder to an app

## Prompt

**The story strip.** *Six panels on warm paper, three across and two down,
each a white card with a green numbered badge in its corner and a short
hand-lettered caption underneath. Panel 1: a folder of paperwork with loose
sheets peeking out and a cursor arrow pointing at it — "You point at a
folder. Invoices, letters, contracts." Panel 2: a small friendly green
figure labelled Elder beside a speech bubble asking three short questions —
"due dates?", "notice periods?", "who may see them?" — captioned "Elder
asks what matters. A few questions — then it offers to spawn a Tribe."
Panel 3: three connected rounded nodes, Partner on top with Contract and
Invoice hanging beneath it — "Atlas pins the ontology. Your things, and how
they relate to each other." Panel 4: a small folder icon and a small
envelope icon, each with a curved green arrow running down into one shared
node labelled Partner — "Two sources, one thing. Both receipts kept."
Panel 5: an anvil with orange sparks flying off it and a freshly made chip
labelled "parser" beside it marked "✦ new tool" — "The Forge fills the gap.
A tool that didn't exist a minute ago." Panel 6: a phone showing a simple
list with a green check mark, noted "on their phone" — "Your partner opens
the app. Nobody wrote code." Bottom line, hand-lettered: "Not an
application you start and stop — a Tribe that takes the domain over."*
